# Engine Convergence & Overlaps
**Baseline:** 2026-11 | **Horizon:** Now -> Dec 31, 2030

Tracks where two or more engines intersect—either amplifying a trend or creating friction, bottlenecks, and policy trade-offs through December 31, 2030.

---

## Core Convergence Zones (Now -> 2030)

### 1. Compute, Power, Grids & Heat (`03 x 05 x 08`)
* **Where They Meet:** AI data-center power demand (~1,000 TWh by 2030 outlook) and summer cooling loads colliding with grid connection queues, transformers, and local water/siting limits.
* **2030 Pressure:** Slower grid interconnects, power-first site selection, behind-the-meter/gas/nuclear deals, and local permitting friction.

### 2. Capital Costs, Tariffs, Minerals & Capex (`01 x 02 x 03 x 04`)
* **Where They Meet:** Elevated interest rates/bond yields, tariffs, and critical-mineral export controls testing debt- and cash-funded AI, grid, and manufacturing buildouts.
* **2030 Pressure:** Higher project hurdle rates, supply-chain workarounds, margin compression, or sharp capex/ROI repricing if returns lag financing costs.

### 3. Chokepoints, Energy & Inflation (`01 x 02 x 04 x 05 x 08`)
* **Where They Meet:** Geopolitical/maritime chokepoints (such as Strait of Hormuz risks), carbon/border rules, and severe weather hitting energy, freight, and food/input costs.
* **2030 Pressure:** Sticky inflation pockets, delayed rate cuts in exposed economies, strategic stockpiling, and multi-fuel energy hedging.

### 4. Affordability, Siting, Trust & Politics (`01 x 06 x 07 x 08`)
* **Where They Meet:** Household cost strain (power bills, housing, insurance, living costs) and local infrastructure/data-center siting meeting low institutional trust and polarized media feeds.
* **2030 Pressure:** Voter backlash, local project vetoes, stop-start policy pivots, and heightened civic friction.

### 5. Demographics, AI Automation & Skill Gaps (`02 x 03 x 06`)
* **Where They Meet:** Rapid aging in economies like Japan, the EU, and China alongside large working-age/youth cohorts in markets like India, while AI reshapes tasks and entry roles.
* **2030 Pressure:** Simultaneous care/labor shortages in aging regions, skill mismatches, faster reskilling demands, and diverging productivity/wage outcomes.
