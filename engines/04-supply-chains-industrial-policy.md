# Engine 04: Supply Chains & Industrial Policy

**Baseline:** 2026-11 | **Horizon:** Now -> Dec 31, 2030 | **Top-5 Minimum Audit:** China, US, EU, Japan, India

## 1. Factors & Forces Tracked (Comprehensive Scope)

* **Critical minerals & refining chokepoints:** Rare earths, gallium, germanium, antimony, graphite, lithium, nickel & cobalt mining, processing, and export controls
* **Industrial subsidies, local content & tariffs:** State manufacturing incentives, domestic procurement/local rules, export licensing/bans, and tariff adjustments
* **Supply-chain diversification & "China+N" moves:** Relocating assembly/component capacity, allied mineral/trade pacts, and dual-sourcing across Asia, North America, and Europe
* **Logistics, shipping lanes & inventory buffers:** Maritime chokepoint disruptions, freight/insurance shifts, buffer stock buildouts, and regionalizing production
* **AI hardware, battery & grid supply bottlenecks:** Packaging/substrates, transformers/grid gear, battery materials, and domestic recycling/refining scaling

## 2. Policies & Moves in Action (Top-5 + Key Shifts)

| Jurisdiction / Actor | Active Policy, Target, or Major Move (Late 2026) | 2030 Impact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **China** | Export-control leverage & temporary suspensions on select critical/dual-use minerals (e.g., extension to Jan 27, 2027) alongside industrial/manufacturing scale | Ongoing leverage over refined minerals & inputs; policy-driven supply uncertainty through 2030 |
| **US** | Domestic mineral/permitting push, Section 232 / trade investigations, allied mineral pacts & tariff/industrial tools aimed at reducing single-source reliance | Stronger push for allied/domestic sourcing & trade friction; higher compliance/sourcing cost |
| **EU** | Industrial/clean-tech localization rules, CBAM import compliance & allied mineral/supply-chain partnerships | Higher compliance & green/sourcing standards for goods entering European markets |
| **Japan** | Supply-chain resilience subsidies, mineral/input diversification & allied tech/material partnerships after severe supply pressure | Deeper diversification away from single-country chokepoints; higher resilience capex |
| **India** | National Critical Minerals Mission, ₹7,280 Cr REPM magnet scheme, MMDR reforms & electronics/component manufacturing push (targeting $500B electronics by 2030) | Faster domestic component/magnet/mineral buildout, while scaling refining & tooling depth |
| **Global Mineral & Trade Pacts** | Nearly 100 export measures on transition minerals since 2020 alongside ~58+ critical mineral partnership agreements signed since 2022 | Fragmented trade rules & bloc-based mineral sourcing replacing pure low-cost globalized trade |

## 3. What Is Happening Now -> 2030 Pressures

* **`04-C1` Export Controls & Suspensions on Critical Inputs** `[2027-2028 Wave]` *(Origin: 2026-11 | Verified: 2026-11)*
`States use export licenses, bans & temporary truces on minerals/dual-use items` -> `Firms face rolling renewal risk, quotas & sudden supply tightness` -> `Higher input volatility, inventory buffers & multi-sourcing costs by 2030`
* **`04-C2` Refining & Processing Concentration Bottlenecks** `[2027-2028 Wave]` *(Origin: 2026-11 | Verified: 2026-11)*
`Mining & refining remain heavily concentrated in a few countries/blocs` -> `New processing & magnet/battery plants take years and heavy capex to permit/scale` -> `Persistent mid-stream bottlenecks in rare earths, battery inputs & specialty materials through 2030`
* **`04-C3` Subsidy Races & Local-Content Rules Fragmenting Trade** `[2027-2028 Wave]` *(Origin: 2026-11 | Verified: 2026-11)*
`Governments deploy grants, tax rules, tariffs & local-content mandates` -> `Companies duplicate capacity or reshuffle assembly across regions` -> `Higher unit manufacturing costs, overcapacity pockets & bloc-aligned supply chains by 2030`
* **`04-C4` Route Disruptions & "Just-in-Case" Inventory Shifts** `[2027-2028 Wave]` *(Origin: 2026-11 | Verified: 2026-11)*
`Geopolitical & maritime chokepoint risks threaten transit times and freight` -> `Importers hold higher safety stock and diversify lanes/suppliers` -> `Higher working-capital/logistics costs & slower, more resilient supply networks by 2030`
* **`04-C5` Recycling & Circular Material Scaling** `[2029-2030 Wave]` *(Origin: 2026-11 | Verified: 2026-11)*
`Export curbs on scrap/black mass & high primary-mineral costs spur domestic recovery` -> `Battery recycling, urban mining & secondary refining scale up` -> `Partial relief on raw-material import dependence by 2029–2030, though primary supply still dominates`

## 4. Fault Lines (Engine Collisions)

* **Engine 04 x Engine 01 & 02:** Mineral/export-control truces & tariffs vs. inflation/rates -> renewed cost spikes or supply halts if truces lapse
* **Engine 04 x Engine 03 & 05:** Bottlenecks in magnets, batteries, grid equipment & chips vs. AI/energy buildouts -> slower clean-energy, EV, grid, or hardware rollouts
* **Engine 04 x Engine 08:** Resource extraction, refining & recycling vs. environmental/permitting rules -> slower mine/plant approvals and higher compliance costs
