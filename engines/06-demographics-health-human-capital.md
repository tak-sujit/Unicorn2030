# Engine 06: Demographics, Health & Human Capital

**Baseline:** 2026-11 | **Horizon:** Now -> Dec 31, 2030 | **Top-5 Minimum Audit:** India, China, US, EU, Japan

## 1. Factors & Forces Tracked (Comprehensive Scope)

* **Population aging & dependency ratios:** Share aged 60+ reaching 1 in 6 people globally (1.4 billion) by 2030, rising old-age dependency, pension/eldercare strain, and shrinking working-age cohorts in aging economies
* **Working-age supply, youth bulge & migration:** Young/working-age cohort expansion in markets like India vs. labor contraction in Japan/EU/China, plus cross-border talent/worker mobility
* **Healthcare workforce & system capacity:** Global health worker shortfall projected around ~11.1 million by 2030 alongside chronic disease, mental health, and eldercare demand
* **Skills, reskilling & education-to-work fit:** AI/automation reshaping entry and mid-level roles, technical/STEM/care skill gaps, and adult retraining speed
* **Public health resilience & longevity:** Preventive care, infectious/environmental health risks, workplace health, and healthy-aging lifespans

## 2. Policies & Moves in Action (Top-5 + Key Drivers)

| Jurisdiction / Actor | Active Policy, Demographic Shift, or Major Move (Late 2026) | 2030 Impact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **India** | Large working-age/youth base paired with skilling, digital health, and workforce expansion pushes | Large labor & talent pool by 2030, conditional on job creation, quality education & skill matching |
| **China** | Rapid population aging, delayed-retirement adjustments, and eldercare/healthcare cost pressures | Shrinking workforce & higher eldercare/pension load; stronger automation & robotics pull |
| **US** | Aging workforce, healthcare cost/access strain, and shifting immigration/work-permit & skill rules | Tight labor supply in key care/service/skilled tiers & higher healthcare/benefit costs |
| **EU** | Aging populations, labor shortages in health/trades/tech, and targeted talent/mobility & green/digital skill programs | Persistent labor/skill bottlenecks & higher pension/health spending pressure through 2030 |
| **Japan** | Deepest aging profile (~30% already aged 60+), labor shortages across care/services, and active senior/foreign-worker & automation adaptation | Acute labor tightness & high eldercare demand; ongoing push for labor-saving tech & longer working lives |
| **Global Health & Work** | WHO *Workforce 2030* tracking an ~11.1M health worker shortfall by 2030 & UN Decade of Healthy Ageing (midpoint 2026) | Care bottlenecks & rising chronic/eldercare costs across both aging and fast-growing economies |

## 3. What Is Happening Now -> 2030 Pressures

* **`06-C1` Rapid Aging & Rising Eldercare / Pension Burden** `[2027-2028 Wave]` *(Origin: 2026-11 | Verified: 2026-11)*
`1 in 6 people globally aged 60+ by 2030 (~1.4B) alongside lower birth rates in many economies` -> `Higher demand for healthcare, long-term care & pensions relative to active taxpayers` -> `Tighter public budgets, higher family/caregiver load & eldercare service growth by 2030`
* **`06-C2` Shrinking Working-Age Pools vs. Youth Job & Skill Gaps** `[2027-2028 Wave]` *(Origin: 2026-11 | Verified: 2026-11)*
`Aging economies face shrinking labor supply while younger markets face large entry cohorts` -> `Simultaneous labor shortages in aging regions and job/skill competition in growing ones` -> `Wage/hiring friction, migration/offshoring shifts & urgent reskilling needs by 2030`
* **`06-C3` Healthcare & Caregiver Shortfalls Under Chronic Demand** `[2027-2028 Wave]` *(Origin: 2026-11 | Verified: 2026-11)*
`Projected ~11.1M health worker gap by 2030 meets rising chronic & age-related conditions` -> `Longer wait times, burnout & higher out-of-pocket or system care costs` -> `Greater reliance on digital health, task-shifting & preventive/home care models by 2030`
* **`06-C4` AI & Tech Reshaping Skill Half-Lives & Career Ladders** `[2027-2028 Wave]` *(Origin: 2026-11 | Verified: 2026-11)*
`AI and automation compress routine tasks across white-collar and operational roles` -> `Traditional entry rungs shift while demand rises for domain, analytical & judgment skills` -> `Faster skill turnover, wider pay/productivity gaps & continuous-learning pressure by 2030`
* **`06-C5` Longevity & Preventive Health Repricing Work/Retirement** `[2029-2030 Wave]` *(Origin: 2026-11 | Verified: 2026-11)*
`Longer lifespans meet chronic disease, heat/health stressors & later retirement norms` -> `Employers and insurers adjust retirement ages, health coverage & workplace ergonomics` -> `Multi-generational workforces & longer working spans becoming standard by 2030`

## 4. Fault Lines (Engine Collisions)

* **Engine 06 x Engine 02 & 01:** Aging/pension/health costs & migration/talent rules vs. fiscal space & labor markets -> budget squeezes, wage pressure, or political pushback
* **Engine 06 x Engine 03:** AI/automation & health-tech scaling vs. human skills & care access -> faster productivity where workers reskill, friction where care/jobs lag
* **Engine 06 x Engine 08:** Heatwaves, air/water stress & disease shifts vs. older or outdoor/low-income cohorts -> higher heat/health strain and care demand
