# Engine 05: Energy, Resources & Physical Infrastructure

**Baseline:** 2026-11 | **Horizon:** Now -> Dec 31, 2030 | **Top-5 Minimum Audit:** US, China, EU, India, Middle East / Gulf

## 1. Factors & Forces Tracked (Comprehensive Scope)

* **Grid networks, interconnect queues & transformers:** Transmission/distribution capex (~$550B globally in 2026), grid connection backlogs, and equipment bottlenecks
* **Energy storage & firm/dispatchable capacity:** Utility battery storage (over $100B spending in 2026), pumped/long-duration storage, and backup/peaking balance
* **Oil, LNG & chokepoint supply shocks:** Strait of Hormuz / Middle East shipping disruptions, gas supply risk (~$330B gas investment in 2026), and strategic buffers
* **AI data-center & electrification load:** Data-center power scaling toward ~1,000+ TWh by 2030, high-density racks, behind-the-meter/gas/nuclear deals, and EV/heat electrification
* **Coal, nuclear & renewables balance:** Renewables/clean power buildouts, nuclear/SMR interest, and continued coal/gas capacity (~$180B coal investment in 2026)
* **Energy security, affordability & siting friction:** Power/fuel bill sensitivity, local permitting resistance, and self-generation/storage hedging

## 3. What Is Happening Now -> 2030 Pressures

* **`05-C1` Grid & Transformer Bottlenecks Lagging Load Growth** `[2027-2028 Wave]` *(Origin: 2026-11 | Verified: 2026-11)*
`AI data centers, electrification & renewables raise peak/net power demand while grid capex scales (~$550B in 2026)` -> `Transmission lines, transformers & interconnect queues lag new load` -> `Slower connections, higher congestion/tariff costs & on-site power workarounds by 2030`
* **`05-C2` Chokepoint & Geopolitical Shocks in Oil & LNG** `[2027-2028 Wave]` *(Origin: 2026-11 | Verified: 2026-11)*
`Middle East conflict & Hormuz shipping risks disrupt energy transit and trade routes` -> `Higher freight/risk costs, inventory draws & importer caution on gas/LNG` -> `Recurring price/supply volatility & stronger push for domestic/diverse energy by 2030`
* **`05-C3` Battery Storage Scaling Alongside Firm/Thermal Capacity** `[2027-2028 Wave]` *(Origin: 2026-11 | Verified: 2026-11)*
`Falling battery costs & >$100B storage spend meet variable renewables and peak demand` -> `2-hour to 4-hour storage & pumped hydro expand while coal/gas/nuclear backstop firm load` -> `Better solar/wind shifting by 2030, yet thermal/firm power remains vital where storage lags`
* **`05-C4` AI Data Centers Driving Gas, Nuclear & Grid Repricing** `[2027-2028 Wave]` *(Origin: 2026-11 | Verified: 2026-11)*
`Data-center electricity demand doubles toward ~1,000+ TWh by 2030` -> `Tech & power developers contract gas turbines, nuclear/SMRs & dedicated power` -> `Higher power-market competition, turbine/equipment tightness & regional tariff friction by 2030`
* **`05-C5` Dual-Track Energy Transition (Clean Scale vs. Fossil Resilience)** `[2029-2030 Wave]` *(Origin: 2026-11 | Verified: 2026-11)*
`Clean/grid investment (~$2.2T in 2026) and fossil/security spending (~$1.2T in 2026) advance together` -> `Security worries slow pure fossil phase-outs while efficiency, EVs & renewables grow` -> `Oil demand leveling/plateauing near ~103 mb/d by 2030 in STEPS alongside sustained multi-fuel mix`

## 4. Fault Lines (Engine Collisions)

* **Engine 05 x Engine 03 & 08:** AI compute + heat cooling demand vs. grid capacity & extreme heat -> power rationing, interconnect delays, or turbine/cooling bottlenecks
* **Engine 05 x Engine 01 & 02:** Hormuz/regional conflict & energy shocks vs. inflation/rates -> cost-push inflation, energy-import strain, or slower rate cuts
* **Engine 05 x Engine 04:** Critical minerals, battery/grid gear & turbine supply limits vs. energy buildouts -> higher capex costs or slower deployment timelines
