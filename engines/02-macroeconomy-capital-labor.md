## 3. What Is Happening Now -> 2030 Pressures

* **`02-C1` Energy & Cost Pass-Through Keeping Rates Elevated** `[2027-2028 Wave]` *(Origin: 2026-11 | Verified: 2026-11)*
`Elevated oil/energy and freight/input costs hit prices` -> `Central banks hold or hike rates to contain second-round inflation` -> `Higher cost of capital, tighter credit for weaker borrowers & delayed easing by 2027–2028`
* **`02-C2` Debt-Fueled AI & Infra Capex vs. Yield / ROI Hurdles** `[2027-2028 Wave]` *(Origin: 2026-11 | Verified: 2026-11)*
`Massive AI & infrastructure spending financed via cash flows, debt & higher bond yields` -> `Rising financing costs & fiscal deficits test investment returns` -> `Capital reallocation, credit stress in leveraged sectors & sharper downcycle risk if ROI disappoints by 2028–2030`
* **`02-C3` Two-Speed Growth (AI/Tech & Exports vs. Domestic/EM Drag)** `[2027-2028 Wave]` *(Origin: 2026-11 | Verified: 2026-11)*
`AI hardware/compute & select regions lead while China domestic demand & energy-importing EMs face headwinds` -> `Uneven corporate earnings, FX/rate pressure & trade/demand gaps` -> `Bifurcated global growth & higher vulnerability for energy-import-heavy economies through 2030`
* **`02-C4` AI & Automation Reshaping Jobs, Hiring & Skills** `[2027-2028 Wave]` *(Origin: 2026-11 | Verified: 2026-11)*
`Employers adopt AI, digital tools & automation (86% of surveyed firms expect AI transformation by 2030)` -> `Cautious hiring, role redesign & rising demand for technical/AI skills` -> `Skill mismatches, slower entry-level hiring in exposed tasks & productivity gains in adopters by 2030`
* **`02-C5` Shrinking Fiscal Cushions Under High Debt & Spending Demands** `[2029-2030 Wave]` *(Origin: 2026-11 | Verified: 2026-11)*
`Higher interest rates, defense/security outlays & public debt limit budget flexibility` -> `Governments face tighter fiscal trade-offs or political gridlock (e.g., debt limits)` -> `Less fiscal stimulus room in the next downturn & greater reliance on private capital/efficiency by 2030`
