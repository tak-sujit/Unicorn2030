# Engine 01: Geopolitics, Security & Governance

**Baseline:** 2026-11 | **Horizon:** Now -> Dec 31, 2030 | **Top-5 Minimum Audit:** US, China, EU, India, Russia

## 1. Factors & Forces Tracked (Comprehensive Scope)

* **Tariff & trade-weaponization cycles:** Retaliatory duties, Section 301 / national-security tariffs, export controls, and shifting trade negotiations
* **Active conflicts & maritime chokepoints:** Eastern Europe/Ukraine attrition, Middle East regional escalation & energy/shipping choke points, and Indo-Pacific/Taiwan Strait/South China Sea flashpoints
* **Defense buildup & alliance burden-shifting:** Rising defense budgets, joint rearmament financing, and shifts in US force posture abroad
* **Multipolar blocs & strategic autonomy:** BRICS expansion/chairships, plurilateral hedging, and non-aligned/multi-aligned trade & energy deals
* **Domestic political & voter constraints:** Cost-of-living/tariff pushback, local infrastructure/AI siting resistance, and election/legislative shifts
