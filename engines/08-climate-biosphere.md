# Engine 08: Climate & Biosphere

**Baseline:** 2026-11 | **Horizon:** Now -> Dec 31, 2030 | **Top-5 Minimum Audit:** EU, China, US, India, Brazil

## 1. Factors & Forces Tracked (Comprehensive Scope)

* **Physical extremes & climate cycles:** Heatwaves, floods, droughts, wildfires, and strong El Niño shifts hitting food and power systems
* **Carbon pricing & trade rules:** Border carbon charges, national carbon markets, and industrial emission intensity rules
* **AI, data centers & cooling:** Surging compute power load, liquid/immersion cooling shifts, grid queues, and behind-the-meter generation
* **Insurance, credit & real estate:** Catastrophe payouts, insurer retreats or rate spikes, mortgage underwriting friction, and municipal budgets
* **Food & commodity inputs:** Crop yields, biofuel demand, fertilizer/feed costs, and export restrictions
* **Public sentiment & political pushback:** Affordability/power-bill backlash, local data-center permitting resistance, and corporate climate commitments

## 2. Policies & Targets in Action (Top-5 + Key Moves)

| Jurisdiction / Actor | Active Policy, Target, or Major Move | 2030 Impact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **EU** | CBAM definitive phase active (Jan 2026); first 2026 declarations due Sept 2027; ETS phase-down & expansion to Norway/Iceland (2027) | Higher carbon costs on covered imports; tighter supply-chain reporting |
| **China** | National ETS expanded to steel, cement & aluminum; multi-year tightening from 2027 | Higher heavy-industry compliance & MRV costs; industrial consolidation |

| **India** | CCTS compliance & carbon credit trading rolling out across heavy energy-using sectors (incl. steel) | Industrial efficiency push & carbon-cost exposure for energy-heavy exporters |
| **Brazil** | COP30 deforestation roadmap (2030 goal) & forest/land-use oversight | Land-use & supply-chain scrutiny; biome moisture tied to crop stability |
| **AI & Hyperscalers** | Global data-center power projected near ~1,000 TWh by 2030; nuclear uprates/deals & liquid cooling scaling | High baseload power & cooling demand; local grid & permitting bottlenecks |
