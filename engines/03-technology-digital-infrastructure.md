# Engine 03: Technology & Digital Infrastructure

**Baseline:** 2026-11 | **Horizon:** Now -> Dec 31, 2030 | **Top-5 Minimum Audit:** US, China, EU, South Korea, India

## 1. Factors & Forces Tracked (Comprehensive Scope)

* **AI compute, model & agent scaling:** Hyperscaler capex, AI cluster/agentic deployments, hardware depreciation cycles, and compute demand vs. power/grid limits
* **Semiconductor & packaging bottlenecks:** Leading-edge foundry nodes, advanced packaging (such as CoWoS), substrates, mature-node capacity, and export/equipment controls
* **Data center & interconnect buildouts:** Grid connection queues, siting/permitting resistance, water/cooling constraints, and subsea/fiber cables
* **AI governance, data & liability rules:** Enforced transparency/synthetic-media rules, staggered high-risk deadlines, product liability, and data-protection enforcement
* **Emerging frontier tech:** Commercial readiness vs. hype in quantum computing, edge/autonomous hardware, and cybersecurity/resilience

## 3. What Is Happening Now -> 2030 Pressures

* **`03-C1` Compute & AI Capex Outrunning Power & Grid Queues** `[2027-2028 Wave]` *(Origin: 2026-11 | Verified: 2026-11)*
`AI & cloud capex surges toward ~945–1,000 TWh data-center power by 2030` -> `Multi-year grid connection queues, cooling/water limits & local siting pushback` -> `Delayed cluster launches, power-first site selection & higher infra costs by 2030`
* **`03-C2` Advanced Packaging & Foundry Concentration** `[2027-2028 Wave]` *(Origin: 2026-11 | Verified: 2026-11)*
`AI accelerators & agentic workloads demand cutting-edge nodes, CoWoS & substrates` -> `Capacity expands across Taiwan, US, South Korea & partners under tight lead times` -> `Persistent supply bottlenecks for top AI silicon & hardware price/allocation shifts by 2030`
* **`03-C3` Fast Hardware/Model Cycles vs. Accelerated Depreciation** `[2027-2028 Wave]` *(Origin: 2026-11 | Verified: 2026-11)*
`Rapid chip generations & model shifts shorten useful life of older compute` -> `Firms face faster equipment obsolescence & rising ROI pressure on heavy capex` -> `Tighter capital discipline, vendor consolidation & refresh cycles through 2030`
* **`03-C4` Phased Global AI & Data Compliance Waves** `[2027-2028 Wave]` *(Origin: 2026-11 | Verified: 2026-11)*
`Transparency, synthetic-media & data rules active in 2026; EU high-risk & India DPDP milestones hit in 2027–2028` -> `Enterprises retrofit logging, labeling, human oversight & data controls` -> `Higher compliance costs, fragmented regional stacks & audit demands by 2030`
* **`03-C5` Bifurcated Tech Stacks & Commercial Shakeout** `[2029-2030 Wave]` *(Origin: 2026-11 | Verified: 2026-11)*
`Export controls, regional AI laws & uneven startup monetization persist` -> `Parallel US/allied vs. China/domestic ecosystems & thin commercial revenue in overhyped niches (e.g., many quantum startups)` -> `Split global standards, M&A/shakeouts & selective breakout winners by 2030`

## 4. Fault Lines (Engine Collisions)

* **Engine 03 x Engine 05 & 08:** Data-center power/water demand + heat cooling vs. grid queues & electricity supply -> delayed interconnects, higher power prices, or local moratoriums
* **Engine 03 x Engine 02:** Trillion-dollar AI/compute capex & depreciation vs. interest rates & ROI hurdles -> capex pullbacks or credit/valuation repricing if returns lag
* **Engine 03 x Engine 01 & 04:** Chip export controls, packaging bottlenecks & regional AI laws vs. global supply chains -> supply-chain friction & split hardware/software ecosystems
