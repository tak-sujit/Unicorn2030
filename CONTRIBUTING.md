# Contributing to Unicorn2030

Contributions to **Unicorn2030** are **optional**. You are welcome to simply read, quote, or adapt the repository under the **CC BY 4.0** license without ever opening an issue or pull request.

If you do want to propose a cause, challenge an assumption, or flag a cross-engine fault line, use the guide below so entries stay consistent with the project's charter.

---

## Core Rules for Any Contribution

1. **2030 Horizon (Now -> Dec 31, 2030):** Focus on structural forces that can shape reality on or before **December 31, 2030** (including skills, jobs, degrees, commodities, inflation, bond yields, supply chains, and regulation). Leave post-2030 speculation or short-term headline noise out.
2. **Causes Over Outcomes:** Do not submit standalone predictions or point-probability guesses. Frame entries as a causal chain:
   `[Active Root Cause] -> [Forcing Mechanism] -> [2030 Structural Pressure]`
3. **Top-5 Policy & Legal Lens:** When proposing a policy or regulatory shift, note which of the **top 5 nations** for that engine it affects and how it acts as a gate or constraint.
4. **Fault Lines Are Welcome:** If you see two engines colliding (for example, power grid limits vs. data center demand), flag the tension rather than averaging it away.

---

## How to Contribute

### 1. Propose a Cause or Flag a Fault Line (Easiest)
* Open a new **Issue** using the **Propose a 2030 Cause or Flag an Exception** template (or start a **Discussion** if you just want to debate an idea).
* Include:
  * **Target Engine (`01` to `08`)**
  * **Active Root Cause** (what is happening now)
  * **Forcing Mechanism** (how it scales or compounds)
  * **2030 Structural Pressure** (how 2030 looks different)
  * **Impact Wave** (`2027-2028 Wave` or `2029-2030 Wave`)
  * **Source / Policy Reference** (link or brief note)

### 2. Suggest an Edit or Monthly Update (Pull Request)
* Fork the repository and make targeted edits to the relevant file under `engines/` or `monthly-digests/`.
* Keep formatting plain Markdown and avoid vague opinions without a causal link.
* Open a **Pull Request** explaining what changed (`+ Add`, `~ Update`, `- Retire`, or `! Flag`) and why.
