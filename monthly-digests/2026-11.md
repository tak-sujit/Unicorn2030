# Monthly Digest — 2026-11
**Baseline:** 2026-11 | **Horizon:** Now -> Dec 31, 2030 | **Status:** All 8 Engines Initialized

## Snapshot Across All 8 Engines
* **01 Geopolitics, Security & Governance:** Tariff pivots, defense rearmament (including EU Readiness 2030 / ReArm Europe), maritime/regional chokepoints, and multipolar hedging.
* **02 Macroeconomy, Capital & Labor:** Elevated rates/bond yields, sticky inflation in energy-importing markets, heavy AI capex vs. debt/ROI hurdles, and AI/automation skill shifts.
* **03 Technology & Digital Infrastructure:** Data-center electricity doubling toward ~1,000 TWh by 2030 under IEA STEPS, grid/packaging/substrate constraints, and phased AI/data rules (2026–2028).
* **04 Supply Chains & Industrial Policy:** Critical-mineral export controls and suspensions, refining concentration, industrial subsidies/local content, and safety-stock shifts.
* **05 Energy, Resources & Physical Infrastructure:** Grid/transformer queues, battery storage scaling alongside coal/gas/nuclear/firm capacity, and Hormuz/Middle East energy chokepoint risks.
* **06 Demographics, Health & Human Capital:** 1 in 6 people aged 60+ by 2030 (~1.4B), ~11.1M projected health workforce gap, aging labor pools vs. youth/skill gaps, and longevity shifts.
* **07 Society, Culture & Information Ecosystems:** Synthetic-media rules/provenance, low institutional trust and polarization, AI-mediated discovery shifting media traffic, and civic cost/siting friction.
* **08 Climate & Biosphere:** EU CBAM definitive phase and national carbon markets, AI cooling/power vs. grids, catastrophe insurance repricing, and extreme heat/weather shocks.

## Top Cross-Engine Fault Lines to Watch
* **Compute & Power vs. Grids & Cooling (03 + 05 + 08):** Data-center load and summer heat colliding with grid connection queues and transformer/cooling limits.
* **Capital Costs & Tariffs vs. Inflation & Capex (01 + 02 + 03 + 04):** Tariffs, mineral controls, and high yields testing debt-funded AI and industrial expansion.
* **Affordability & Siting Friction vs. Policy & Trust (01 + 06 + 07 + 08):** Energy, housing, insurance, and living cost pressures fueling political and local resistance.
